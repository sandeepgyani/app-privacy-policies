Privacy Policy — Jyotish Guru

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Jyotish Guru ("the app") is published by PCS Solutions. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect: none

Jyotish Guru does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and works entirely offline on your device.

Birth details you enter

The name, date, time and place of birth you type are used only on your device, in the moment, to compute astrological charts. They are processed in the app's own web view, are not saved after you close the app, and never leave your phone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app requires no sign-in, shows no advertisements, and contains no analytics or tracking libraries of any kind.

Printing

If you use the print button, the chart is handed to Android's own print service on your device. No data is sent to us.

Disclaimer

The app's astrological calculations and predictions are offered for informational and cultural purposes. They are not professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
