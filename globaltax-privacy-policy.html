Privacy Policy — Global Tax Calculator

App package: in.co.pcssolutions.globaltax · Developer: PCS Solutions · Last updated: 3 August 2026

Summary

Global Tax Calculator is a fully offline educational tool. The app does not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personal data whatsoever.

Data collection

None. The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere. All figures you type (salary, investments, family details) exist only on your screen while the app is open and are discarded when you close it. Nothing is written to your device storage, no account is created, and no identifiers are read.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or advertising identifier.

Third parties, ads and analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party code that receives user data.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Important disclaimer

The app provides simplified, educational estimates of personal income tax for several countries. It is not tax, legal, or financial advice, and PCS Solutions is not affiliated with any government or tax authority. Always verify with official sources or a qualified adviser before making decisions.
