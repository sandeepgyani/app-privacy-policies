Privacy Policy — CostCompass: Cost of Living

Effective date: 3 August 2026 · App package: in.co.pcssolutions.costcompass · Publisher: PCS Solutions (sandeepgyani)

Summary

CostCompass is a fully offline cost-of-living comparison tool. The app does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

Data we collect

None. The figures you enter (age group, family size, income, lifestyle preferences) are used only in your device's memory to calculate estimates while the app is open. They are not saved to disk, not transmitted anywhere, and disappear when you close the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions. It has no access to the Internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking SDKs, and makes no network calls. All country and city reference data is bundled inside the app.

PDF reports

The "PDF Report" feature uses your device's built-in print/save-as-PDF system dialog. Any PDF you create is generated and saved locally by your device; the app never sees or uploads it.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience, collects no data from anyone, and contains no user-generated or social content.

Disclaimer on estimates

All cost-of-living figures are indicative estimates based on bundled reference data. They are provided for general information only and are not financial, tax, immigration or relocation advice. Verify important decisions with official sources.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app ever changes its data practices, this policy will be updated at this URL before that version is released.
