Privacy Policy — Hasta Darpan

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Hasta Darpan ("the app") is published by PCS Solutions. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect: none

Hasta Darpan does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no internet permission and works entirely offline on your device.

Your photographs

The palmistry and face-reading features ask you to choose a photo from your device. That photo is displayed and measured only inside the app on your own phone. It is never uploaded, never sent to any server, and is not saved by the app — it is discarded when you close or reset the screen. The app requests no camera or storage permission of its own; the Android system photo picker hands the app only the single image you choose.

Details you type

Names, dates of birth and any optional numbers you enter for numerology are used only on your device, in the moment, to perform the calculations. They are not stored after you close the app and never leave your phone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app requires no sign-in, shows no advertisements, and contains no analytics or tracking libraries of any kind.

Printing

If you use the print button, the reading is handed to Android's own print service on your device. No data is sent to us.

Disclaimer

Palmistry, face reading and numerology are traditional cultural practices. The app's readings are provided for interest, entertainment and self-reflection only. They are not medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and must not be used as a basis for decisions about health, money, employment or relationships.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
